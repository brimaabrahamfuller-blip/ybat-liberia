Our purpose

Potential is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

YBAT exists to help young people turn energy, imagination, and talent into real pathways forward. Through sports, creative arts, leadership, mentorship, and skills-building activities, we help youth grow with purpose.

Our work is rooted in community, shaped by young people, and built around the belief that every talent deserves a chance to be seen.
